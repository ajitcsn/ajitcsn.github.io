YOUTUBE, ON PURPOSE

Choose your next watch.

YouTube: Add Video to Cart is a browser extension that lets you add a video to Cart before you watch it. When you are ready, check out a finite session that fits your time and attention.

01

Save

Click an unapproved video on YouTube to place it in your Cart rather than starting a new rabbit hole.

02

Choose

Set a video limit, time box, or daily-credit rule. Select only what you intend to watch now.

03

Finish

Watch your active session, mark videos complete, and keep the rest of your Cart for later.

LOCAL BY DEFAULT

Your viewing plan stays in your browser.

Cart entries, active sessions, whitelists, and statistics are stored locally by the extension. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.
